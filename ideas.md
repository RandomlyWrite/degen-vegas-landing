# DEGEN VEGAS Landing Page Design

## Design Philosophy: Rubber-hose Noir

**Chosen Aesthetic:** A dark-humored, semi-chaotic blend of early 1930s rubber-hose Disney-ish animation, the grimy high-stakes casino atmosphere of *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas*, and a twisted, campy Tim Burton aesthetic.

### Core Principles

1. **Scratchy Linework & Cross-hatching:** Hand-drawn, imperfect linework creates authenticity and personality—nothing feels sterile or corporate.
2. **Muted Teal & Warm Golds:** The color palette is desaturated and grimy, with warm amber/gold accents for neon signs and UI elements.
3. **Button-eyed Characters & Surreal Elements:** Whimsical, unsettling figures populate the scene, balancing humor with an edge of menace.
4. **Semi-lit Neon Aesthetic:** Flickering, partially burnt-out signs create mood and focus without overwhelming the composition.

### Color Philosophy

- **Primary Background:** Deep charcoal/black with subtle texture (smoke, grain)
- **Neon Accents:** Warm coral/pink for "DEGEN VEGAS" sign; cool teal for secondary neon ("SLOTS," "DICE")
- **UI Elements:** Weathered brass, burnt copper, muted gold for buttons and borders
- **Text:** Cream/off-white for high contrast against dark backgrounds

**Emotional Intent:** Gritty, high-stakes, darkly humorous—a place where bad decisions happen with a wink.

### Layout Paradigm

- **Centered Hero:** The main entrance/marquee dominates the vertical center
- **Card Border Frame:** Playing card corner elements (Queen of Clubs) frame the entire viewport
- **Scrolling Marquee:** Leaderboard highlights scroll horizontally at the top
- **Bottom CTA Zone:** Two distinct buttons ("Verify Fairness" and "Enter the Lounge") anchor the bottom

### Signature Elements

1. **Flickering Neon Signs:** Semi-lit, weathered marquee with some letters burnt out
2. **Grimy Smoke & Haze:** Atmospheric smoke effects blur the background
3. **Scratchy Typography:** Hand-drawn, imperfect fonts for headings
4. **Playing Card Corners:** Decorative Q♣ elements frame the layout

### Interaction Philosophy

- **Sound Toggle:** Subtle audio cue when toggled (optional)
- **Button Hover States:** Buttons glow slightly, neon intensifies on hover
- **Marquee Animation:** Continuous smooth scroll with leaderboard data
- **Entrance Feel:** The page should feel like standing outside a high-stakes casino, about to make a questionable decision

### Animation

- **Marquee Scroll:** Smooth, continuous horizontal scroll (8-12s per loop)
- **Button Glow:** Subtle neon glow intensifies on hover (200ms ease-out)
- **Smoke Effects:** Gentle, slow-moving background animation (very subtle)
- **Entrance Fade:** Page elements fade in on load (300-500ms staggered)

### Typography System

- **Display Font:** Bold, scratchy serif (e.g., "Courier Prime" or hand-drawn style) for "DEGEN VEGAS"
- **Subheading Font:** Monospace or slab serif for "HIGH STAKES. LOW STANDARDS."
- **Body/UI Font:** Clean sans-serif (e.g., "Courier New" or "IBM Plex Mono") for marquee and buttons
- **Hierarchy:** Large, bold display → medium subheadings → small monospace for data

### Brand Essence

**One-liner:** A darkly comedic gateway to high-stakes gambling for degens who know the house always wins—and love it anyway.

**Personality Adjectives:** Gritty, Irreverent, Theatrical

### Brand Voice

- **Headlines:** Provocative, self-aware, darkly funny
- **CTAs:** Direct, slightly menacing, tongue-in-cheek
- **Microcopy:** Sarcastic, witty, never corporate

**Example Lines:**
- "High stakes. Low morals."
- "Fortune favors the degen."
- "You're in the right place. Probably not for the right reasons."

### Wordmark & Logo

A bold, scratchy "DEGEN VEGAS" text with a playing card symbol (Queen of Clubs) integrated into the design. The mark should feel hand-drawn, imperfect, and slightly unsettling—like it was designed by someone who just lost big.

### Signature Brand Color

**Warm Coral/Pink (#E85D5D or similar):** Used for the primary neon sign. This color is unmistakably DEGEN VEGAS—warm, slightly faded, and nostalgic.
