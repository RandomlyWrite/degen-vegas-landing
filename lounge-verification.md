# DEGEN VEGAS Lounge Verification

The poster landing page rendered with the Enter the Lounge hotspot. Activating it transitioned into the new lounge without a route reload.

The lounge exposes:
- Leave the Door
- Connect Wallet and wallet rail
- Demo chip stack with explicit no-on-chain-wagering copy
- High / Low Degen Dice
- Neon Roulette
- Rekt Craps Table
- Per-game controls and Play for Demo Chips action
- Table Tape history and Copy Player ID

The default demo session starts at 250 virtual chips and no wallet connection. No wallet signature or transaction is requested by the implemented demo connector.

The default High / Low Degen Dice play action updated the virtual chip stack from 250 to 275 after a simulated roll of 9 and appended a result to Table Tape. Switching to Neon Roulette replaced the console content, choices, title, and house-rule copy without leaving the lounge.
